# Heavy Metal GP v1.18.0 - The Grand Scrapyard Expansion

### 🔨 Workshop Expansion: 30 New Items & Universal Handles
- 30 new building pieces and hazards across 6 complete sets:
  - **Shortcuts & Secrets**: Barricades, Cliff tunnels, Crumbling walls, Trapdoors (Clock & Weight)
  - **Blades & Crushers**: Saw blades (with track slider handles), Swinging blades, Crusher pistons, Rolling boulders, Mace sweepers
  - **Mechanical Movers**: Water wheels, Screw lifts, Conveyor belts, Reversed belts, Seesaws, Rope bridges
  - **Launchers & Pinball**: Goblin cannons, Catapults, Left & Right flippers, War Drums, Scoops
  - **Fields & Surfaces**: Updraft vents, Horseshoe magnets, Tar bands, Geyser vents
  - **Big Set Pieces**: Trampoline nets, Turnstile diverters, Drop-target banks, Vortex funnels, Moving platforms
- **Universal Transform Handles**: Interactive rotate and resize handles on every single piece in the editor!

### 🏆 Multiplayer Ranks & Elo Ladder
- Competitive Elo-based rating system with 6 tiers: Scrap (0+), Bronze Bolt (1100+), Iron (1250+), Steel (1400+), Gold Gear (1550+), and Heavy Metal (1750+).
- Ranked matchmaking pairs drivers with similar skill, climbing the global RUN.world ladder.

### 💬 Multiplayer Live Chat & Speech Bubbles
- In-game real-time chat with fellow drivers during online races.
- Live speech bubbles appear directly above player marbles on track, backed by a pit wall chat console.
